Security architecture

Privacy isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

Every layer of Nexora is built with security-first principles — from how we encrypt your QR to how we verify identity without storing it.

AES-256 Encryption

All QR payloads and communication channels are encrypted with 256-bit AES before transmission.

Blockchain Verification

QR identity tokens are anchored on-chain to prevent tampering, spoofing, and replay attacks.

JWT Authentication

Stateless JSON Web Token authentication with short-lived tokens and secure refresh flows.

Privacy-Preserving QR

QR codes contain only an anonymised token — no personal data is embedded or readable by scanners.
